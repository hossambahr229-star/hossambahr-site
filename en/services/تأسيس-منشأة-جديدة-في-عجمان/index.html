Required documents

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

Residents: security clearance.

Trade-name reservation certificate and licence application.

Valid passport and ID for the owner and all partners, as applicable.

Common conditions — category eligibility not fully verified

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

The card addresses individuals and businesses and lists security clearance for residents. Activity-specific and legal-form eligibility and external approvals still require confirmation with Ajman DED.

Government fees

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

Official card items in AED: licence 600; administrative application 50; signboard permit 350; commercial registration 200; unified activities registration 200; signboard specification approval 100; register certificate 200; community contribution 1500; LLC instrument publication 3000. Contract notarisation is shown as 50–50000 depending on capital; English translation 50 per page; chamber membership 500–5000. These are conditional items, not a combined total or quotation. Confirm applicable charges with Ajman DED before payment. No HOSSAM BAHR fee is included.

Processing time

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

10 minutes for licence issuance according to the Ajman government card; not total company setup or completion of prior approvals.

How to proceed

  1. Sign in using UAE Pass.
  2. Submit the application and required documents.
  3. Pay applicable charges.
  4. Receive the commercial licence.
