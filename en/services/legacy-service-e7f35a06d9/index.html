Which hospitality transaction do you need?

Choose the transaction first. Hotel classification, operator registration and a holiday-home unit permit are different stages; unit-permit facts do not apply to hotels.

Classify a hotel establishment

Requirements, fees and steps

Documents

  • DET initial approval, licence-holder passport, land map, municipality building-completion certificates and trade-name certificate.

Government fees for this stage

Variable by hotel category, star rating and number of units; the card gives no universal amount.

Processing scope

Application processing within one business day, with a classification visit; this is not a deadline for all hotel operating licences.

Steps

  • Prepare property details, sign in to the classification portal, complete the application and optional self-assessment, and prepare for the DET visit.

Issue a new holiday-home unit permit

Requirements, fees and steps

Documents

  • Title deed or DLD-approved sale agreement; developer NOC where applicable; landlord and signatory identity/passport; company-landlord licence; management letter; current DEWA bill.
  • A representative signing the management letter needs a power of attorney and passport. Whole-building deeds may require unit-specific evidence.

Conditions

Enter the DEWA premise number rather than the account number. Operator permit dates match the management letter; display the permit inside the unit.

Government fees for this stage

AED 300 per bedroom, plus AED 50 classification certificate, AED 10 knowledge and AED 10 innovation. The card also shows AED 370; do not treat it as every unit's total. A yearly AED 1,200 cap is stated; confirm its application and the payable total in the portal.

Processing scope

Application review within one business day, followed by self-classification and payment after approval.

Steps

  • Sign in, add the unit, provide its details and documents, and review the declaration. After approval, complete self-classification and pay.
