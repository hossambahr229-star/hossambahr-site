Issuance of a New Work Permit - Mission Work Permit

Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation · Federal UAE

This is the platform's English service title. It does not replace the authority's official title or requirements.

Official source limits

The reviewed Arabic card identifies the quota as a mission-permit requirement. The English condition calls it temporary work; this guide follows the Arabic service-specific wording. Permit validity must not be confused with processing time.

Required documents

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

Colour photograph on a white background.

Passport copy valid for at least six months.

The ministry job-offer form signed by employer and worker.

Attested qualifications by skill level: degree for 1–2, diploma or higher for 3–4, secondary certificate for 5; no certificate for 6–9. Skilled status also requires the stated qualification and monthly salary threshold of AED 4,000.

A professional licence from the competent authority for an occupation requiring one.

Eligibility and conditions

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

Age 18 or older and no active work permit. The occupation must match the establishment activity. A valid establishment licence without violations suspending operations and an application by its authorised signatory are required. Mission permits require an electronic quota. Regulated occupations require their professional licences.

Government fees

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

Published federal fee: AED 50. Website/app use adds no ministry service charge above government fees. Tax and collection charges are excluded. A business centre may separately charge up to AED 72; this is not a government fee or a HOSSAM BAHR fee.

Processing time

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

Processing: two working days. Permit validity: three months, extendable once for the same period.

How to proceed

  1. Sign in with UAE Pass through an approved channel.
  2. Complete and review the application information and attach the required documents.
  3. Review the application and pay the prescribed fees.
  4. The permit is issued after requirements are met; additional information may be requested.
