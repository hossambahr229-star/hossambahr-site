Security Industry Regulatory Agency (SIRA) · Dubai

This is the platform's English service title. It does not replace the authority's official title or requirements.

Ask AI about this serviceRead the official source ↗Open the government service ↗

Required documents

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

Business establishments: trade-name reservation and initial approval receipt, or trade licence; free zones use the initial approval request letter as listed.

Buildings: plot affection plan or trade licence.

Approved security plan stamped by the consultancy and installation company, and a successful security audit report.

Eligibility and conditions

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

Dubai establishments and buildings, according to the applicable category. An approved security plan is required before requesting the audit. Do not operate before a successful audit report; check the activity or building-specific requirements.

Government fees

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

AED 100 service fee, AED 10 knowledge and AED 10 innovation according to the card. Other security work or services are not included.

Processing time

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

Two business days according to the service card; not an approval guarantee.
