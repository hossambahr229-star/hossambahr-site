Official source limits

This MOHRE source is a Taqyeem programme guide, not a direct application or a complete transaction card. It describes starting the assessment visit through a Tasheel centre, which forwards the request electronically to a Taqyeem provider. Case-specific documents, government charges and completion time are not published on this page.

Before you continue

The available source explains the programme. Confirm the following for your transaction with the service channel before applying.

  • Documents Not verified in this guide.
  • Eligibility and conditions Not verified in this guide.
  • Government fees Not verified in this guide.
  • Completion time Not verified in this guide.

How to proceed

  1. Confirm the required assessment type with a Tasheel service centre.
  2. Start the Taqyeem request through a Tasheel centre; the guide describes electronic referral to a Taqyeem provider.
  3. The assessment team visits the establishment following referral. Confirm your case-specific documents, charges and timing with the channel before applying.
