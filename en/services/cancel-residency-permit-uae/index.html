Eligibility and conditions

HOSSAM BAHR explanatory translation of the recorded Arabic details. This is not the authority's official English wording.

The official GDRFA service covers cancellation of all Dubai-issued residence-permit types. Do not use it for an ICP-issued residence in another emirate.
