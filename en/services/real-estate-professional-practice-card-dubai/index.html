Eligibility and conditions

HOSSAM BAHR explanatory translation of the recorded Arabic details. This is not the authority's official English wording.

Dubai Police good-conduct certificate and annual brokerage test, subject to official exemptions. Card validity follows the licence; valuer and trainee conditions also apply.

Read the recorded Arabic text

حسن سيرة من شرطة دبي واختبار الوساطة السنوي مع الاستثناءات الرسمية. صلاحية البطاقة مرتبطة بالرخصة؛ توجد شروط إضافية للمُقيّم والمتدرب في البطاقة.

Government fees

HOSSAM BAHR explanatory translation of the recorded Arabic details. This is not the authority's official English wording.

AED 500 for most cards and AED 5,000 for a valuer card. Exam, knowledge, innovation and ERES fees depend on category; check the official total.

Read the recorded Arabic text

500 درهم لمعظم البطاقات و5,000 لبطاقة مُقيّم العقارات. رسوم الاختبار والمعرفة والابتكار وERES تضاف بحسب الفئة؛ تحقق من الإجمالي الرسمي.

How to proceed

  1. Open the official source linked above.
  2. Confirm the transaction, emirate and applicant type.
  3. Review the authority's current documents, fees and conditions.
  4. Apply through the channel specified by the authority. Sign-in may be required.
  5. Keep the application reference and track the authority's decision.
