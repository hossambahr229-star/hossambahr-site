Required documents

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

Evidence explaining the absence beyond six months.

Eligibility and conditions

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

Apply abroad after 180 days; residence remaining over 30 days; valid reason. Enter within 30 days including approval date. Excludes Dubai. Source exemptions: valid Golden/Green/Blue residence and a UAE woman's husband sponsored by her.

Government fees

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

AED 100 per delay month after six months, plus 100 smart services. Conditions specify each 30 days or less; confirm calculation.

Processing time

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

2 days; not an approval guarantee.

How to proceed

  1. Apply abroad through ICP.
  2. Provide the reason/evidence and review data.
  3. Pay applicable fees.
  4. Track the decision and approved entry deadline.
