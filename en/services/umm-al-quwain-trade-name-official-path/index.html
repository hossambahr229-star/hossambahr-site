Required documents

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

For UAE or GCC citizens: original ID, passport copy, email and telephone number of the licence holder.

For the expatriate director in charge: original ID, passport and residence copies, email and telephone number.

Certified sponsor NOC or investor licence copy in the cases stated on the official card, with additional branch-manager requirements where the manager differs.

Three written trade names indicating the activity in Arabic, translated into English.

Power of attorney if the relevant person is absent.

Printed requested activities and the licence-related email and telephone number.

Confirm whether the licence follows the Sheikh Saud Youth Projects programme.

Eligibility and conditions

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

Documents vary by nationality, director and branch status, and representation by power of attorney. Review the official card's applicable cases. Reserving a trade name is not licence issuance. The fee field says “on demand” without an amount, and no completion time is published; confirm both with the department.
