Official source limits

The ATLP card quotes AED 68.53 without separating government and provider components. It is not published as a government fee or a HOSSAM BAHR charge. MOHRE labour-file cancellation differs from ICP/GDRFA immigration establishment-card cancellation. The former Ministry news link described obtaining a cancellation statement, not submitting the cancellation itself.
