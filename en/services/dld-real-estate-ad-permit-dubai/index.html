Dubai Land Department / Real Estate Regulatory Agency · Dubai

This is the platform's English service title. It does not replace the authority's official title or requirements.

Required documents

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

Copy of the advertisement form — For advertisements, billboards and promotional campaigns.

Event-site evidence where applicable — Site tenancy contract or hotel NOC depending on the permit type.

Platform design or seminar documents where applicable — Platforms: a design showing dimensions and the site contract. Seminars: room booking and a topics letter.

List of participating real estate companies — For real estate exhibition organization permits only.

Documents vary by advertisement type; the official DLD card lists the exact requirements for each type.

Eligibility and conditions

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

A company or entity holding the appropriate real estate activity licence in Dubai.; If the applicant is a real estate broker, a marketing contract with the property owner is required for the ad types listed on the official card.; Exhibition documents must be submitted at least one month in advance, and sales are prohibited during the exhibition; only preliminary reservations are allowed.; Exhibitions from the same country need a 30-day gap. Dubai off-plan properties must be registered with the Escrow Account Department before display.

Government fees

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

Project launch event permit 5000 AED — An AED 20 Knowledge and Innovation fee is added.; Other permits 1000 AED — An AED 20 Knowledge and Innovation fee is added.; Exhibitions have a non-refundable AED 10,200 preliminary booking fee and AED 1,020 per exhibitor; the booking fee is deducted from the final permit fee.

Processing time

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

One working day.

How to proceed

  1. Open the official source linked above.
  2. Confirm the transaction, emirate and applicant type.
  3. Review the authority's current documents, fees and conditions.
  4. Apply through the channel specified by the authority. Sign-in may be required.
  5. Keep the application reference and track the authority's decision.
