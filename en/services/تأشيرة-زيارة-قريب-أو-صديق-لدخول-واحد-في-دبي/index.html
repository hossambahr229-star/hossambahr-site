Required documents

HOSSAM BAHR explanatory translation of the recorded Arabic details. This is not the authority's official English wording.

Two personal photographs and a passport copy valid for at least six months.

Marriage contract and birth certificate according to the required proof of relationship.

Salary certificate for government, semi-government and free-zone entities, employment contract for private companies, or partnership contract for a partner.

Home-country identity card for certain nationalities: Iraq, Pakistan, Iran and Afghanistan.

Eligibility and conditions

HOSSAM BAHR explanatory translation of the recorded Arabic details. This is not the authority's official English wording.

The visitor must be a relative or friend of a UAE citizen or resident, with proof of the relationship, reasons for the visit and payment of the required security deposit. A foreign wife visiting a GCC-national husband must hold valid residence in the country of the husband's nationality.

Government fees

HOSSAM BAHR explanatory translation of the recorded Arabic details. This is not the authority's official English wording.

AED 200 for 30 days, AED 300 for 60 days or AED 400 for 90 days, plus 5% VAT. Security deposit of AED 1,000, AED 20 guarantee service fee and AED 40 collection and return fee. Medical insurance is AED 40, AED 60 or AED 90 according to the duration. If the sponsored person is inside the UAE: AED 10 Knowledge Dirham, AED 10 Innovation Dirham and AED 500 inside-country fee. The total may vary by case.
