Required documents

HOSSAM BAHR explanatory translation of the recorded Arabic details. This is not the authority's official English wording.

Valuation application form.

Owner's letter and valid Emirates ID or passport.

Municipality map valid for one year, or planning map.

Recent property photographs.

Property-specific additional documents, including areas, contracts and financial statements where applicable.

نموذج طلب التقييم.

خريطة بلدية سارية لسنة أو خريطة تخطيط.

صور حديثة للعقار.

مستندات إضافية بحسب نوع العقار، ومنها المساحات والعقود والبيانات المالية عند انطباقها.

Government fees

HOSSAM BAHR explanatory translation of the recorded Arabic details. This is not the authority's official English wording.

Published base fees range from AED 2,000 to AED 15,000 by valuation type, plus applicable knowledge, innovation and partner fees; no universal total.

Read the recorded Arabic text

Processing time

How to proceed

  1. Open the official source linked above.
  2. Confirm the transaction, emirate and applicant type.
  3. Review the authority's current documents, fees and conditions.
  4. Apply through the channel specified by the authority. Sign-in may be required.
  5. Keep the application reference and track the authority's decision.
