Eligibility and conditions

HOSSAM BAHR explanatory translation of the recorded Arabic details. This is not the authority's official English wording.

The passport must be valid for more than six months. Investors or partners must present the establishment card. A government-sector sponsor must provide a profession-change letter; a private-sector sponsor must provide an employment contract or employment ID.

Government fees

HOSSAM BAHR explanatory translation of the recorded Arabic details. This is not the authority's official English wording.

The card lists AED 100 for urgent service, AED 10 innovation fee, and AED 100 printing fee when submitted through ZAJEL Courier Center. This text does not establish the total fee for every case.
