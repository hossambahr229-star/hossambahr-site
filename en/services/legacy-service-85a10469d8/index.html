Government fees

HOSSAM BAHR explanatory translation of the recorded Arabic details. This is not the authority's official English wording.

Published permit fees in AED, by type: promotional campaigns 500–20,000 depending on the store area; sales 2,500–13,500 by sale type; special offers and liquidation 2,500; exhibitions 500–1,000 by duration; conferences 250–1,000 by type; commercial tents 5,000–15,000 by area; banners from 250 by size. These are alternatives, not one total. This review adds no HOSSAM BAHR or external-approval fee.

Processing time

HOSSAM BAHR explanatory translation of the recorded Arabic details. This is not the authority's official English wording.

The Invest in Dubai card states 5 minutes to complete the commercial permit request through service centres or the portal. This is not a guarantee for an external approval or every permit type; confirm the request type and requirements with DET.

Read the recorded Arabic text

تذكر بطاقة Invest in Dubai إنجاز طلب التصريح التجاري خلال 5 دقائق عبر مراكز الخدمة أو البوابة. ليست ضمانًا لإصدار موافقة خارجية أو لكل نوع تصريح؛ أكد نوع الطلب ومتطلباته لدى DET.
