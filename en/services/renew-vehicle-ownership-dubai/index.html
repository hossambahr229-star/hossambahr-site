Required documents

HOSSAM BAHR explanatory translation of the recorded Arabic details. This is not the authority's official English wording.

Valid vehicle insurance verified electronically by RTA.

Pass the technical inspection when required; the inspection centre updates its result.

Vehicle and Traffic File details to sign in and select the vehicle.

تأمين مركبة ساري تتحقق منه RTA إلكترونيًا.

اجتياز الفحص الفني عند طلبه وتحديث نتيجته عبر مركز الفحص.

بيانات المركبة والملف المروري للدخول وتحديد المركبة.

Eligibility and conditions

HOSSAM BAHR explanatory translation of the recorded Arabic details. This is not the authority's official English wording.

For vehicles registered in Dubai. Ownership is valid for one year; pass the safety test and settle fines and fees. The system verifies insurance and inspection; new-vehicle inspection exemptions and case-specific conditions may apply.

Read the recorded Arabic text

للمركبات المسجلة في دبي؛ الملكية صالحة لسنة، ويلزم اجتياز فحص السلامة وتسوية المخالفات والرسوم. يتحقق النظام من التأمين والفحص، وقد تنطبق استثناءات الفحص للمركبات الجديدة وشروط إضافية بحسب الحالة.

Government fees

HOSSAM BAHR explanatory translation of the recorded Arabic details. This is not the authority's official English wording.

RTA displays the final fees by vehicle category, plate option and delivery; late fees apply where relevant. There is no universal total.

Read the recorded Arabic text

تعرض RTA الرسوم النهائية حسب فئة المركبة وخيار اللوحة والتوصيل، وتطبق رسوم تأخير عند انطباقها؛ لا يوجد إجمالي موحد لجميع الحالات.

Processing time

How to proceed

  1. Open the official source linked above.
  2. Confirm the transaction, emirate and applicant type.
  3. Review the authority's current documents, fees and conditions.
  4. Apply through the channel specified by the authority. Sign-in may be required.
  5. Keep the application reference and track the authority's decision.
