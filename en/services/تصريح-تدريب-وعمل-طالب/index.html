Official source limits

The Arabic card confirms the student permit, rather than the juvenile permit wording used in one English quota condition. The prohibited-work clause must not be read as a blanket permission for dangerous work. Published fees are alternatives, not an automatically calculated total. No HOSSAM BAHR fee is added.

Required documents

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

Colour photograph on a white background and Emirates ID copy.

Proof of student status; a valid residence visa for a non-national student.

Written guardian or custodian consent for a student aged 15–18 as specified by the card.

Medical fitness certificate from a competent authority.

Educational-institution NOC for training.

Ministry contract signed by the employer and student.

Eligibility and conditions

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

The Arabic description specifies students aged at least 15. The establishment must not be suspended and must have a valid licence without violations suspending operations. The occupation must match its activity, the authorised signatory applies, and no other active work permit may exist. No insurance, bank guarantee or electronic quota is required. Juvenile and dangerous-work restrictions apply; confirm any profession-specific exception with MOHRE rather than treating it as permission for dangerous work.

Government fees

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

Federal fee: AED 50. Website/app use adds no ministry service charge above government fees. Business-centre commission is separate, capped at AED 72, and is not a government or HOSSAM BAHR fee. Tax and collection charges are excluded.

Processing time

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

Two working days. Permit validity: three months.

How to proceed

  1. Open the official source linked above.
  2. Confirm the transaction, emirate and applicant type.
  3. Review the authority's current documents, fees and conditions.
  4. Apply through the channel specified by the authority. Sign-in may be required.
  5. Keep the application reference and track the authority's decision.
