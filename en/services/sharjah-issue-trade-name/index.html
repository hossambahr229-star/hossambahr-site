Eligibility and conditions

HOSSAM BAHR explanatory translation of the recorded Arabic details. This is not the authority's official English wording.

The name is valid for three months and renewable. It must not be misleading, prohibited or conflict with a registered trademark or name.

Read the recorded Arabic text

صلاحية الاسم ثلاثة أشهر قابلة للتجديد، ويجب ألا يكون مضللًا أو محظورًا أو متعارضًا مع علامة أو اسم مسجل.

Government fees

HOSSAM BAHR explanatory translation of the recorded Arabic details. This is not the authority's official English wording.

AED 250 for a trade name, AED 1,250 for a translated name, and AED 2,250 for a foreign name, according to the service card.
