Official source limits

Published fees are alternatives, not an automatically calculated total. No HOSSAM BAHR fee is added.

Required documents

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

Ministry contract signed by the employer and worker.

Qualifications by skill level: degree for 1–2, diploma or higher for 3–4, secondary certificate for 5; none for 6–9. Skilled status also requires the stated qualification and monthly salary of at least AED 4,000.

A competent-authority professional licence where the occupation requires one.

Eligibility and conditions

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

The authorised signatory applies. The establishment licence must be valid without violations suspending operations. A new occupation must match its activity; regulated occupations retain their professional licensing requirements.

Government fees

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

Federal fee: AED 50. Website/app use adds no ministry service charge above government fees. Business-centre commission is separate, capped at AED 72, and is not a government or HOSSAM BAHR fee. Tax and collection charges are excluded.

Processing time

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

Two working days.

How to proceed

  1. Open the official source linked above.
  2. Confirm the transaction, emirate and applicant type.
  3. Review the authority's current documents, fees and conditions.
  4. Apply through the channel specified by the authority. Sign-in may be required.
  5. Keep the application reference and track the authority's decision.
