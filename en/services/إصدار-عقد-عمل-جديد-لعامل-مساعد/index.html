Required documents

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

Employer passport copy and citizen employer unified-number page, as applicable.

Domestic worker passport copy and colour photograph.

Medical insurance copy; adequate insurance is specified for Abu Dhabi and Dubai.

Domestic employment contract signed by both parties.

Eligibility and conditions

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

Valid employer passport and ID, valid residence for a resident/investor employer, worker passport valid for at least three months and a valid medical fitness result.
