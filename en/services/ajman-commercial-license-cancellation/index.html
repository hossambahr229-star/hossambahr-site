Required documents

HOSSAM BAHR explanatory translation of the recorded Arabic details. This is not the authority's official English wording.

Identity evidence for the parties concerned.

Termination of the company's partnership agreement.

Newspaper notice for the prescribed period.

Auditor and general-assembly letters for companies, where applicable.

Eligibility and conditions

HOSSAM BAHR explanatory translation of the recorded Arabic details. This is not the authority's official English wording.

The notice period is 15 days for some establishments and 45 days for the specified companies. Complete liquidation documents according to the legal form.

Read the recorded Arabic text

مدة الإعلان 15 يومًا لبعض المنشآت و45 يومًا للشركات المحددة، مع استكمال مستندات التصفية بحسب الشكل القانوني.

Government fees

HOSSAM BAHR explanatory translation of the recorded Arabic details. This is not the authority's official English wording.

AED 2,000 to cancel the licence of a non-operating establishment, AED 50 for the application, and AED 500 for the notice, plus certificate and notarization fees where applicable.

Read the recorded Arabic text

2,000 درهم لإلغاء رخصة منشأة متوقفة، و50 درهمًا للطلب، و500 درهم للإعلان، مع رسوم الشهادة والتوثيق عند انطباقها.

Processing time

HOSSAM BAHR explanatory translation of the recorded Arabic details. This is not the authority's official English wording.

10 minutes after the notice and liquidation requirements are complete.

Read the recorded Arabic text

How to proceed

  1. Open the official source linked above.
  2. Confirm the transaction, emirate and applicant type.
  3. Review the authority's current documents, fees and conditions.
  4. Apply through the channel specified by the authority. Sign-in may be required.
  5. Keep the application reference and track the authority's decision.
