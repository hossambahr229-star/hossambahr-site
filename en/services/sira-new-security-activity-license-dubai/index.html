Security Industry Regulatory Agency (SIRA) · Dubai

This is the platform's English service title. It does not replace the authority's official title or requirements.

Ask AI about this serviceRead the official source ↗Open the government service ↗

Required documents

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

Trade licence, or trade-name reservation and initial approval inquiry receipt, or a free-zone NOC, as applicable.

Additional requirements and security staff depend on the activity code in SIRA's official table.

Eligibility and conditions

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

Dubai security activities only. Meet the activity-specific security or personnel conditions before operating. This is new issuance; renewal, amendment and cancellation are distinct transactions.

Government fees

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

The card lists AED 100 service, AED 10 knowledge and AED 10 innovation fees, alongside separate activity-specific amounts. AED 120 is not a universal activity licence total; check the activity code and final official amount.

Processing time

English details from the platform's recorded source. Check the linked authority for current requirements.

Two business days according to the service card; not an approval guarantee.

How to proceed

  1. Open the official source linked above.
  2. Confirm the transaction, emirate and applicant type.
  3. Review the authority's current documents, fees and conditions.
  4. Apply through the channel specified by the authority. Sign-in may be required.
  5. Keep the application reference and track the authority's decision.
