Government fees

HOSSAM BAHR explanatory translation of the recorded Arabic details. This is not the authority's official English wording.

AED 200 for 60 days, AED 300 for 90 days or AED 400 for 120 days, plus 5% VAT. Security deposit of AED 1,000, AED 20 guarantee service fee and AED 40 collection and return fee. If the sponsored person is inside the UAE: AED 10 Knowledge Dirham, AED 10 Innovation Dirham and AED 500 inside-country fee. The total may vary by case.
