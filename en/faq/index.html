Help and frequently asked questions

Is HOSSAM BAHR a government authority?

No. HOSSAM BAHR is an independent service. Government authorities make approval, visa and licensing decisions.

Why does the emirate matter?

The responsible authority and transaction pathway can depend on the emirate and issuing authority. Dubai residence and federal ICP pathways must be distinguished.

Are the displayed fees final?

Published fees may depend on your case. The amount shown by the responsible authority before payment is the final reference. Hossam Bahr assistance fees are separate and agreed before execution.

Does the platform store personal data?

General browsing does not require an account. If you create an account, save a request or upload a document in the protected workspace, the platform processes that information to provide those features. Read the privacy page before sharing personal data.

What if my request is unclear?

The assistant may ask for the emirate or another detail to identify the correct transaction. Missing verified information must remain visible rather than being guessed.

How do I check service information?

Open the service details and linked official source. Review the displayed verification status and any missing information before applying.
