It preserves specific recorded Arabic descriptions. English descriptions now identify the real service, jurisdiction and authority, with honest wording about available information.

The canonical service body, H1, links, execution paths and design are byte-preserved. Existing canonical/hreflang, social image ownership, aliases, noindex/protected pages and all203records remain intact. Open Graph/Twitter use the same final title/description. BreadcrumbList is derived only from the actual visible breadcrumb links and H1. No FAQ-rich-result claims, fake reviews, keyword stuffing, invented local offices, new services or mass doorway pages are introduced.
