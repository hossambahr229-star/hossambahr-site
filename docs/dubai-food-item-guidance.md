# Dubai food item guidance

The bilingual catalog entry for registering and assessing a food item uses the public Dubai Municipality directories. The detailed application card (service 2974) did not respond during verification. The published entry is official guidance; the application channel, documents, eligibility, fees and processing time are not marked as verified. Premises layout approval (2976) remains separate.

Sources: [Arabic municipal services](https://www.dm.gov.ae/dubai-municipality-services/?lang=ar) and [Dubai Municipality food safety directory](https://www.dm.gov.ae/municipality-business/food-safety-department-2/important-information-to-food-establishment/).
